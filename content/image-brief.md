# Website Image Brief

The photography should make the site feel current, credible, and human. It should show Ryan as an executive who stays close to the work, not as a stock-photo version of a consultant. Supply original, unedited files when possible. The final crops and compression can be handled in the site.

## Hero portrait

- **Preferred source size:** 2400 by 3000 pixels
- **Minimum source size:** 1600 by 2000 pixels
- **Crop:** Vertical 4 by 5
- **Intent:** A current environmental executive portrait with natural light and direct presence. A medium shot or wide head-and-shoulders composition is better than a conventional corporate headshot.
- **Composition:** Keep Ryan near the center or slightly right of center. Leave enough space around the head and shoulders for responsive cropping.
- **Setting:** A real working environment, understated architectural setting, or outdoor location with visual depth. The background should support the portrait without becoming the subject.
- **Wardrobe:** Solid charcoal, navy, forest, cream, or another muted tone. Avoid small patterns and visible brand logos.
- **Avoid:** Selfies, phone-camera perspective, event screenshots, heavy artificial blur, crossed-arm executive poses, and busy office backgrounds.

## Speaking and advisory image

- **Preferred source size:** 2400 by 1800 pixels
- **Minimum source size:** 1600 by 1200 pixels
- **Crop:** Landscape 4 by 3
- **Intent:** Ryan actively speaking, facilitating, or working through a decision with a group. The image should show presence and interaction rather than simply prove that a microphone was present.
- **Composition:** Keep Ryan's face and gesture sharp. Include enough audience, stage, workshop wall, or room context to make the moment credible.
- **Avoid:** Low-resolution video stills, strong colored stage lighting, partially cropped faces or hands, empty podium shots, and images dominated by a screen.

## Personal panorama

- **Preferred source size:** 3000 by 1360 pixels or larger
- **Minimum source size:** 2400 by 1100 pixels
- **Crop:** Approximately 2.2 by 1
- **Intent:** A personal sense of place that creates breathing room before the about section. It can reference aviation, the outdoors, travel, or another genuine part of Ryan's life without reading like a vacation photo.
- **Composition:** Keep the horizon level and avoid placing a critical subject near an edge. A person may appear in the frame but should not need to carry the image.
- **Current status:** The Hawaii panorama is technically usable. Replace it only if a better image adds more personal meaning or visual consistency.

## File preparation

- Use JPEG, PNG, HEIC, or TIFF source files in the sRGB color space.
- Do not crop tightly before delivery. Extra frame area makes responsive crops much easier.
- Original files can be large; final website assets will be resized and compressed after selection.
- Use images Ryan owns or has permission to publish.
